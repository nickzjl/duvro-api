This page explains exactly what happens to your photos when you use duvro — in plain words, because there isn't much to explain.

effective august 2026 · duvro for iOS, version 1

Do you see my photos?

Only the ones you pick, as small copies, once — so our AI can choose the post. We don't store them.

Do you sell my data, or run ads?

No. We don't keep anything to sell, and there are no ads or trackers in duvro.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for, so we never learn your name or email.

Can duvro browse my camera roll?

No. The system photo picker shows us only what you choose, and saving back uses Apple's add-only access — duvro can add finished photos, never read your library.

ON YOUR PHONE

what happens on-device

Almost everything. When you import, duvro scores your photos with Apple's on-device Vision framework — measuring things like sharpness, framing, and how well faces are captured. Faces are counted and measured, never recognized: duvro has no idea who anyone is, and no face-recognition technology of any kind.

All editing happens on your phone too. The looks in duvro's vibe packs are rendered locally with Apple's Core Image — your full-resolution photos never leave the device to be edited.

If you're offline, duvro still works: photo selection falls back to a fully on-device method, and nothing is sent anywhere.

THE ONE MOMENT SOMETHING LEAVES

choosing your post

To pick the best photos and write captions, duvro sends small copies of your shortlisted photos — about 512 pixels on the long edge — to our server, along with basic numbers about each one: its category, how many faces it has, when it was taken, and its dimensions.

Our server passes them to Anthropic's Claude, the AI that selects your post and drafts captions. Then everything comes back to your phone.

  • We don't store your photos. They're processed in memory and never written to disk on our side.
  • We don't log them either. Our server is built to never record the contents of requests.
  • They aren't used to train AI. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your photos and captions are not used to train their models.
  • The connection is encrypted (HTTPS), end to end.

WHAT WE NEVER COLLECT

the list is short because there's nothing on it

  • No account, name, email, or phone number — there's no sign-up.
  • No location. duvro doesn't read where your photos were taken.
  • No contacts, no messages, no browsing history.
  • No analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • No access to your photo library — only the photos you hand us through Apple's picker.

Because duvro keeps nothing, there's nothing to delete or export on request — your photos live in your library, under your control. Deleting the app removes everything duvro ever had.

SAVING YOUR POST

add-only, by design

When your post is ready, duvro asks for Apple's add-only photo permission — the narrowest one that exists. It lets duvro add your finished photos to the camera roll, and nothing else: it cannot read, browse, or delete your library.

Your caption is copied to the clipboard on your phone, ready to paste into Instagram. It isn't sent or stored anywhere.

WHO WE WORK WITH

two service providers, no one else

  • Vercel hosts our server. Like any web host, it processes basic connection data — such as IP addresses — to deliver and protect the service.
  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI that selects your photos and drafts captions, under commercial terms that exclude training on your data.

That's the complete list. No ad networks, no data brokers, no analytics companies.
