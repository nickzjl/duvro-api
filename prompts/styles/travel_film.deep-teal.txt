THE LOOK
Cool, cinematic, teal-shadowed. Saturated and vibrant with the strongest
vignette in the app. NOTE this is the ONLY variant anywhere in the app with a
POSITIVE tint — it pushes green, not magenta, which is what produces the teal
cast rather than a plain blue one. That direction was verified empirically and
is deliberate; do not neutralise it.

WHAT BASIC TIER DOES
The deterministic Core Image chain below is the same aesthetic, and premium
must read as that look executed better — not as a different look. These are
the app's own tuned values (duvro/Packs/travel_film.json), hand-graded against
reference images. Op order is
temperatureTint -> colorControls -> vibrance -> toneCurve -> vignette ->
bloom -> grain. Baseline temperature is 6500K, so a lower number is warmer.

temperatureTint  temperature 7151, tint +20   (cool, GREEN — the only positive tint in the app)
colorControls    saturation 1.10, brightness 0.0, contrast 1.0
vibrance         0.20
vignette         intensity 0.16, radius 1.2   (strongest in the app)
bloom            radius 44.8, intensity 0.13
grain            intensity 0.12, size 1.0
