THE LOOK
Early-2000s point-and-shoot with the flash on: the coolest, most saturated and
most vibrant variant in the app, with both the heaviest bloom and the heaviest
grain, plus a small yellow date stamp in the bottom-right corner. Harsh and
nostalgic, deliberately over-processed. NOTE the date stamp is a composite
overlay of the photo's own capture date, not invented text — if the provider
cannot place accurate text, generate the graded image only and let the app
stamp it.

WHAT BASIC TIER DOES
The deterministic Core Image chain below is the same aesthetic, and premium
must read as that look executed better — not as a different look. These are
the app's own tuned values (duvro/Packs/digicam.json), hand-graded against
reference images. Op order is
temperatureTint -> colorControls -> vibrance -> toneCurve -> vignette ->
bloom -> grain. Baseline temperature is 6500K, so a lower number is warmer.

temperatureTint  temperature 8275, tint -20   (coolest in the app)
colorControls    saturation 1.08, brightness 0.0, contrast 1.0
vibrance         0.19
vignette         intensity 0.12, radius 1.2
bloom            radius 102.4, intensity 0.26   (largest bloom in the app)
grain            intensity 0.32, size 0.8       (heaviest grain in the app)
dateStamp        bottom-trailing, scale 0.035, yellow digital numerals
