THE LOOK
The diffused one. Near-neutral temperature, desaturated, and dominated by a
large highlight-concentrated glow — the heaviest bloom in the pack by a wide
margin — with the finest grain. Hazy and luminous. IMPORTANT: the glow must
stay concentrated in the highlights; an even white wash over the whole frame
is the specific failure this variant has already had once (see the pack's
tuning notes), and it is what the shadows staying properly dark prevents.

WHAT BASIC TIER DOES
The deterministic Core Image chain below is the same aesthetic, and premium
must read as that look executed better — not as a different look. These are
the app's own tuned values (duvro/Packs/golden_hour.json), hand-graded against
reference images. Op order is
temperatureTint -> colorControls -> vibrance -> toneCurve -> vignette ->
bloom -> grain. Baseline temperature is 6500K, so a lower number is warmer.

temperatureTint  temperature 6030, tint -28   (closest to neutral 6500K)
colorControls    saturation 0.93, brightness 0.0, contrast 0.98
vibrance         0.05
vignette         intensity 0.05, radius 1.6
bloom            radius 76.8, intensity 0.24   (largest bloom in the pack)
grain            intensity 0.01, size 0.8
